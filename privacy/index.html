Rhino Roster Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Rhino Roster is a scheduling, payroll and billing application operated by Searight Holdings, Inc. for its home care offices. This policy explains what information Rhino Roster handles and what we do with it.

1. Information we handle.

  • Account information: name, email, phone and role for staff, caregivers and family members who sign in.
  • Care and scheduling records: client and caregiver details, visits, clock-in times and locations, and notes needed to deliver care.
  • Billing and payroll records: rates, hours, invoices and payments.
  • QuickBooks Online data, when an administrator connects it: company information, customers, products and services, invoices, payments and related accounting records.

2. How we use it. To schedule and deliver care, pay caregivers, bill clients and payers, meet legal and licensing requirements, and keep the system secure. QuickBooks data is used only to create invoices and payments and keep balances matched between Rhino Roster and QuickBooks.

3. What we do not do. We do not sell personal information or QuickBooks data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share QuickBooks data with anyone outside our company, other than the service providers in section 4.

4. Who we share with. Service providers that host and run the system for us, under contracts that require them to protect the data. Payers and government agencies, when billing or the law requires it. Others only with consent or when the law requires it.

5. Health information. Client health information is protected under HIPAA and is covered by our Notice of Privacy Practices. Care details are not sent to QuickBooks.

6. Security. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to authorized users by role, and access is logged. QuickBooks access tokens are stored encrypted.

7. Retention. We keep records as long as needed to run the business and as the law requires. When an administrator disconnects QuickBooks, we delete the stored access tokens and stop reading QuickBooks data. Accounting records already saved in Rhino Roster stay as part of our business records.

8. Your choices. You may ask to see or correct the information we hold about you by contacting the office. Some records must be kept by law.

9. Changes. We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
